Map Design Guide


Requirements:
- All levels must be tested and solvable.
- All levels must be in landscape format, meaning the width is greater than the height.
- All levels must take inspiration from the layouts and puzzles found in the original 50 Sokoban levels.
- All levels must be defined in the SOK format.
- All levels must have a playful title of 2-4 words that matches the game theme.


Considerations:
- Goal tiles are often grouped together in a level, but may also be distributed across the level in smaller groups.
- A good variation between the levels, within the constraints given below, should be prioritized.
- Within each level collection, the levels are sorted by difficulty (number of boxes, length of the solutions).


Easy Levels:
- Number of levels: 10
- Number of boxes per level: 2-4
- Width of map: 6-12
- Height of map: 4–8
- Solution length: 10-100


Medium:
- Number of levels: 20
- Number of boxes per level:  4-8
- Width of map: 8-16
- Height of map: 6-12
- Solution length: 20-200


Hard:
- Number of levels: 30
- Number of boxes per level: 8-16
- Width of map: 10-20
- Height of map: 8-16
- Solution length: 40-400
